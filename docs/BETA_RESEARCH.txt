Beta research

Beta-only content is enabled only when the detected executable profile explicitly
declares it.
Bloom and Doom 0.1.1.1014

This build retains the most experimental content, including beta-only entity
IDs, discarded Crazy Dave behavior, and additional projectile sources. The dog
zombie and bee/propeller zombie are restricted to this profile.
Beta 0.9.9.1029

This profile supports partial beta research, including Limbo and the Squirrel
mode. Some assets and internal mappings remain incomplete.
Squirrel

The Squirrel minigame is partially reconstructed in beta 0.9.9.1029. Loading and
limited testing are available, but counters, nut contents, rewards, and safe
restart are not complete. Bloom and Doom retains related artwork, but its mode
mapping is less certain.

Final releases keep some related progress fields but do not retain the required
artwork and are therefore not offered Squirrel support.
Experimental content

Discarded plants, zombies, projectiles, and modes are classified per build as
functional, partial, or visual-only. Final builds never receive beta IDs merely
because their memory layout looks similar.
