Playable title screen concept

Status: design idea only. This is not implemented and does not write to the
original game's process or save file.
Interactive Title Garden

Turn the presentation screen into a tiny, self-contained lawn while preserving
the original logo, music, and menu:

• The cursor begins as a glove. Clicking the logo makes it drop a sun token.
• Collecting the first sun grows a sunflower beside the Adventure sign.
• Small non-attacking zombies occasionally cross the foreground. Clicking a
  menu stone places a temporary plant that pushes them back.
• Each original menu item remains a normal, reliable button. Its plant or prop
  is only an animated visual wrapper around the same action.
• Completing a 20-second interaction reveals one harmless historical fact,
  beta sprite, or Almanac card. Nothing is permanently unlocked in the save.
• A Skip animation control and reduced-motion option keep the menu usable.
Safe implementation boundary

This should be a separate scene or overlay owned by the trainer, not a patch
that converts the game's title screen into a real board. A small state machine
can manage intro, interactive, opening_menu, and finished states. Asset
selection must be build-aware: final releases use only final assets, while
confirmed beta art is exposed only after an exact beta identity match.

The experiment should never alter Adventure progress, inventory, or the active
GameMode. Closing the overlay returns to the untouched title screen.
